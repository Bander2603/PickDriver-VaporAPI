Account Deletion Request

Last updated: March 18, 2026

This page lets you start your PickDriver account deletion request outside the app, in line with Google Play requirements.

1. Recommended option (from the app)

  • Open PickDriver.
  • Go to Profile -> Delete account.
  • Confirm the operation.

2. Outside-the-app option (web/email)

If you cannot access the app, send a request from your account email to: support@pickdriver.cc

Start request by email

3. What gets deleted

  • User account and app access.
  • Associated profile data (username/email).
  • Game and notification links associated with the account.

4. What might be retained temporarily

We may temporarily retain minimal information when necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

5. Processing time

We aim to process deletion requests as quickly as possible after validating account ownership.

Important: use the same email address that you use in PickDriver to speed up validation.
